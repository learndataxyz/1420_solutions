## Education operations need systems that keep pace with student and staff demands

ERPNext gives education organizations a stronger base for admissions, fee collection, staff management, and academic operations. We customize it around the way your institution really works.

### Key challenges we solve

- Manual admissions and fee collection workflows that create delays
- Staff HR and payroll processes disconnected from student records
- Timetables and resource plans that change too often to track in spreadsheets
- LMS or external systems that do not exchange data cleanly

### Core modules and features

- Student admissions and records
- Fee and billing management
- HR and payroll for staff
- Timetable and resource planning
- LMS integration and operational reporting

### Example outcome

A school or college using ERPNext with 1420 Solutions can reduce administrative handoffs, improve fee visibility, and bring HR, student records, and reporting into one connected system.
