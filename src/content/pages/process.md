## How we work

Our methodology keeps the project grounded in operational outcomes rather than software deliverables alone.

### Step 1 — Discovery

We map workflows, pain points, and business requirements.

### Step 2 — Selection & Scoping

We define the ERPNext modules, custom features, and integrations that fit the real use case.

### Step 3 — Implementation

We configure the system, migrate data, and connect the supporting tools your team needs.

### Step 4 — Training

We help the business adopt the system with hands-on learning and practical guidance.

### Step 5 — Support & Reporting

We stay involved with ongoing support, maintenance, and quarterly or yearly reporting.
