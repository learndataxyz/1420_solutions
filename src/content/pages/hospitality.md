## Stop losing revenue to double-booked rooms and manual housekeeping logs

Hospitality teams need tighter control over reservations, room readiness, purchasing, and guest service. We configure ERPNext so front-of-house and back-of-house teams work from the same live operating picture.

### Key challenges we solve

- Room inventory and reservations managed across disconnected tools
- Housekeeping status not reflected in occupancy and service teams
- Inventory and procurement decisions made without live usage visibility
- POS and operational systems that do not talk to one another

### Core modules and features

- Front desk and reservation management
- Housekeeping workflows and room readiness tracking
- Inventory and procurement controls
- POS integration and shift reporting
- Multi-property management support

### Example outcome

A multi-property hospitality business can align reservation data, housekeeping activity, procurement, and sales reporting in one operational system, reducing missed service opportunities and manual follow-up.
