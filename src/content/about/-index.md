1420 Solutions helps organizations select, implement, customize, and support ERP systems tailored to the way their industry actually operates. Our foundation is ERPNext, but we do not stop at installation. We extend the platform with custom software, automation, AI tools, and data analysis services that connect directly to the real workflows your team uses every day.

### What makes us different

* We position ourselves as an implementation partner, not just a software vendor.
* We work from the operational reality of each industry, not from a generic template.
* We stay involved after go-live with training, support, and reporting services.
* We build the missing layers that ERPNext alone cannot handle out of the box.

Our goal is simple: help businesses run with better visibility, fewer manual workarounds, and systems that support growth instead of slowing it down.

### Our approach

We start with discovery, then define the modules, integrations, and custom features required for your operation. Once the system is live, we keep the relationship practical and long-term through support, reporting cadence, and ongoing performance reviews.

This model makes 1420 Solutions a credible technology partner for operationally complex businesses that need software to fit the process, not the other way around.
