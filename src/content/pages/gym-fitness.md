## Memberships, classes, and billing should run without manual cleanup

For gyms and fitness operators, growth often creates operational friction: class schedules, memberships, trainer capacity, and renewals all need to stay in sync. ERPNext can become the operational backbone for memberships, staff, and facility management.

### Key challenges we solve

- Membership renewals and billing tracked across multiple spreadsheets
- Class scheduling that does not reflect trainer plans or room availability
- Weak visibility into attendance and utilization trends
- Access control or other hardware systems that are not integrated cleanly

### Core modules and features

- Membership and subscription management
- Class scheduling and attendance tracking
- Billing and payment workflows
- Trainer and equipment operations
- Access control integrations where needed

### Example outcome

A fit-out or franchise-style gym operation can reduce billing friction, improve class capacity planning, and keep staff and member data aligned in one system.
