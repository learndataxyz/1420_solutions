## Projects move fast. Your systems should keep up.

Real estate and construction businesses need visibility into budgets, vendors, project progress, and sales or leasing workflows. ERPNext can help unify those moving parts so every site and every transaction stays connected.

### Key challenges we solve

- Project budgets and actual costs tracked separately from the source of truth
- Supplier and contractor activity disconnected from project status
- Listings, leads, and lease or sales progress losing momentum in manual records
- Site progress and spend visibility not available to leadership in time

### Core modules and features

- Project and site tracking
- Contractor and vendor management
- Budgeting and cost control
- Property listings and lease or sales tracking
- Procurement and materials coordination

### Example outcome

A property development or construction operation can improve site-to-finance visibility, tighten cost control, and reduce the effort required to coordinate vendors and budgets.
