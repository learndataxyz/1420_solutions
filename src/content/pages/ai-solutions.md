## AI that solves a specific problem — not a vague promise

We build AI tools that plug directly into your ERPNext data so your team can automate repetitive operations and catch issues earlier.

### Where AI helps

- Staff spending hours manually entering data from invoices, forms, or records
- Stockouts or demand shifts that catch the team by surprise
- Support tickets or claims piling up faster than staff can review them
- Billing or purchase mismatches discovered too late to fix cheaply

### What we build

- Document processing and data extraction
- Predictive analytics for stock-outs and demand changes
- Support and operations chatbots trained on your data
- Anomaly detection for billing errors and workflow exceptions

### Built on your existing data

Our AI tools are designed to read from and write back to your ERPNext instance so the work happens where your team already operates.
