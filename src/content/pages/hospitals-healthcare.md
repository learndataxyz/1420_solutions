## Healthcare operations need accuracy, speed, and consistent compliance.

Hospitals and healthcare providers need systems that can handle patient records, billing workflows, insurance claims, inventory, and staff scheduling without creating operational bottlenecks. ERPNext can be configured to support those needs and extended with custom tooling where necessary.

### Key challenges we solve

- Billing and insurance claims processed with manual follow-up
- Patient records and external systems not aligned in one workflow
- Pharmacy or medical supply inventory visibility that is too slow or fragmented
- Compliance and reporting requirements spread across too many tools

### Core modules and features

- Patient record support and EHR or EMR integration
- Billing and insurance claims workflows
- Inventory for pharmacy and medical supplies
- Staff scheduling and resource planning
- Compliance and reporting support

### Example outcome

A healthcare organization using ERPNext with 1420 Solutions can reduce billing friction, improve inventory visibility, and give teams a more reliable operational structure for compliance and reporting.
